1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to function correctly and, where permitted, to collect statistical information about its use.

2. Cookies used on this website

Essential storage: this website stores your language preference and your cookie choice in your browser. This storage is strictly necessary for the site to work as expected and does not require consent.

Analytics cookies: if analytics is enabled by the site owner, these cookies help us understand how visitors use the website. They are only activated after you accept them.

Marketing cookies: if marketing measurement is enabled by the site owner, these cookies are used to measure the performance of advertising campaigns. They are only activated after you accept them.

3. Managing your preferences

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link in the footer, or by deleting cookies and site data from your browser settings.

4. Third-party services

Where analytics or marketing services are activated, the corresponding providers act as independent or joint controllers according to their own policies. The specific providers in use must be listed here before publication.
